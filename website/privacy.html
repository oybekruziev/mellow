Privacy Policy

Short version: Mellow has no account, no analytics and no tracking. Your tasks, plans and flowers stay on your Mac. Nobody, including me, can see them.

Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Mellow app for Mac and the website bemellow.cc. Both are made and owned by Oybek Ruziev (“I”, “me”). See the Terms for who owns Mellow and how it is provided.

The Mellow app

What stays on your Mac

Everything you type or earn in Mellow is saved only on your Mac, in the app's own preferences file:

  • your task names, plans and timer lengths;
  • today's finished sessions and flowers;
  • your settings, such as the companion, sounds, music and appearance.

This data is never uploaded, synced or shared. Mellow has no servers that could receive it.

What the app sends over the internet

One thing only: about every six hours, and when you choose Check for Updates…, Mellow asks GitHub whether a newer version exists (api.github.com/repos/oybekruziev/mellow/releases/latest). The request says “Mellow” and the version number you run. It contains nothing about you or your tasks.

If you choose to install an update, Mellow downloads it from GitHub and checks its signature before it opens. Like any website, GitHub sees your IP address when this happens. GitHub's own privacy statement covers that.

The lofi music is built into the app and plays offline. Links in Settings open in your browser only when you click them.

What the app never does

  • No account, sign-in or email address.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No access to your files, contacts, camera, microphone or location.

Deleting your data

To remove everything Mellow saved, quit Mellow, move it to the Trash and delete ~/Library/Preferences/uz.oybek.Mellow.plist.

The website, bemellow.cc

Visit and download counts

To know how many people visit and download Mellow, the site keeps simple counts. It uses no cookies and stores no IP addresses. For each day it saves:

  • the number of page views and DMG downloads;
  • a random-looking code made from your IP address and browser, mixed with a secret that changes every day. It lets one person count once a day, and it can't be turned back into your IP or linked to you on another day;
  • your country, as Cloudflare reports it from your connection;
  • the website that linked you here, if your browser shares it (for example google.com).

Only I can see these numbers, on a password-protected page. They are never sold, shared or used for advertising. Known bots are not counted.

Hosting

The site and the download run on Cloudflare. Like every host, Cloudflare processes your IP address to deliver pages and protect the site from attacks. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Other services on the page

  • The GitHub star count is fetched by the site's own server, so your browser doesn't contact GitHub for it.
  • The “Featured on MacBase” badge in the footer is an image loaded from macbase.app, which sees a normal image request.
  • Links to GitHub, Instagram, X, LinkedIn, Threads, YouTube and other sites follow those sites' own policies once you open them.

Children

Mellow collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If Mellow ever starts handling data differently, this page changes first and the date at the top shows when.
