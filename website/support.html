Getting started

What do I need?

A Mac with macOS 26 Tahoe or later. Mellow is free.

How do I install Mellow?

Download Mellow.dmg, open it and drag Mellow into Applications. Open it from Applications. Mellow lives in the menu bar, so it has no Dock icon.

macOS says it can't check the app. Is it safe?

Every release is signed and notarized by Apple. If you see a warning, make sure you downloaded Mellow from bemellow.cc or GitHub, then open it again from Applications. Never install a copy from another site.

How do I update?

Mellow checks for new versions every few hours and tells you when one is ready. You can also choose Check for Updates… from the Mellow menu in the menu bar. Each update is verified before it installs.

Using Mellow

Where did the panel go?

Click the time or the Mellow icon in the menu bar and choose Show Panel. ⌘W hides it and ⌘M shrinks it to a small capsule.

Which keyboard shortcuts are there?

Space starts or pauses, ⌘P opens the plan, ⌘M toggles the capsule, ⌘. ends the session, ⌘L focuses the task field and ⌘, opens Settings. Press 1, 2 or 3 to pick a preset length before you start.

Is my data backed up or synced?

No. Mellow keeps everything on your Mac only and has no account or cloud. A Time Machine backup of your Mac includes it.

How do I uninstall Mellow?

Quit Mellow from the menu bar, move it from Applications to the Trash, and to remove your data too, delete ~/Library/Preferences/uz.oybek.Mellow.plist.
