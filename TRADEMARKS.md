- the blossom logo and the app icon
- the mascot artwork (plant, cat, candle, fox, coffee, moon, cactus): every growth stage,

You will find these files in `Mellow/Resources/Assets.xcassets` (the logo, app icon and mascot
image sets), `Mellow-handoff/Generated/`, and in `website/` (`assets/logo.png`, `assets/flower.png`,
`assets/companions/`, `assets/sprites/`, `og-image.jpg`, `favicon*`, `apple-touch-icon.png`).

## What you can do

- Build and run Mellow from source, change it, and learn from the code.
- Fork it and publish your changes, as long as a fork you distribute uses **its own name,
  icon and mascots**. Please replace the files listed above before you ship.
- Show screenshots of Mellow in reviews, articles, talks and lists.
- Send improvements back as pull requests. Mellow itself is welcome to use them.

## What needs permission

- Distributing an app, website or other product named Mellow, or using the logo, icon or
  mascots in it.
- Using the artwork on its own, for example as stickers, merchandise or in another app.

Ask at r.oybek2001@gmail.com.
